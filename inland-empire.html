Tile, solar and pigeons

What we find on Inland Empire roofs

The tracts built across the Inland Empire in the last twenty years came with tile roofs, and a lot of them came with solar. That combination is exactly what pigeons look for: a warm, shaded pocket under the panels with a view of the street. In the newer parts of Fontana, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario and Eastvale, it is common to see several roofs on one block with birds under the array.

Pigeon proofing is the job we are known for up the hill, and it is the same job down here. Nests, droppings and debris come out and get hauled away, the area is sanitized so the smell goes with them, the panels are washed, and rust resistant mesh is clipped to the frame. Nothing is drilled, so your panel warranty is safe.

The Inland Empire is a different market in a few ways. The tracts are denser, more associations have rules about what shows on a roof, and there is less hard water on the glass than in the desert. None of that changes the price. You get the same numbers as a customer in Hesperia, and we plan our Inland Empire days so the drive never shows up on your bill.

Inland Empire questions

      


      

        

          Do you charge extra to come down the hill?
          
No. The Inland Empire gets the same published prices as Hesperia and Victorville, with no travel fee and no minimum drive charge. We group jobs down here onto scheduled route days, so the drive is our problem, not yours.

        

        

          Which Inland Empire cities do you cover?
          
Fontana is part of our regular area. By request, on scheduled route days: Rancho Cucamonga including Etiwanda and Alta Loma, Ontario, Upland, Chino Hills, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Corona, Norco, Rialto, San Bernardino, Highland, Redlands, Yucaipa and San Jacinto. If you are close to any of those, type your ZIP into the quote tool and it will tell you.

        

        

          How soon can you get to me?
          
Usually within the week, on a day we are already booked in your area. Send your quote online, pick morning or afternoon or say you prefer weekends, and Tony texts back with the soonest day.

        

        

          Is the pigeon warranty the same down here?
          
Yes. Lifetime workmanship warranty in writing, rust resistant mesh clipped to the panel frame with nothing drilled, and 2 to 3 reflective spinners installed free, exactly like a High Desert job.

        

        

          My HOA is strict about what shows on the roof. Is that a problem?
          
Usually not. The mesh sits flush with the panel line in black or gray, the spinners mount on existing vents, and nothing is bolted to the roof. If your association wants a certificate of insurance or a description of the work first, we send one.

        

        

          Does pigeon proofing include the solar wash and the droppings cleanup here too?
          
Yes. Every pigeon proofing job includes washing the panels and cleanup of the droppings under and around them, the same as up the hill.
