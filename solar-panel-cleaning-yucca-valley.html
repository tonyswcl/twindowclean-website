        The wash
        
How a basin array gets cleaned

      

      

        
Purified water is pumped up a pole to a soft brush. The brush loosens the film, the pure water carries it off, and because there are no minerals in the water the panels dry with nothing on them. Nobody steps on a module. If we notice anything visible, like a cracked panel, lifted flashing, or feathers and droppings at the array edge that mean birds have moved in, you hear about it before we leave. If it is work we do, like pigeon proofing, you get a price first.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Basin arrays
        
Three kinds of systems out here

      

      

        

          
Grid tied tract arrays

          
Arrays on the newer Yucca Valley and Twentynine Palms homes, often facing two ways. A 24 panel wash is $168, and paired with the windows on the same stop the visit clears the $249 minimum.

        

        

          
Off grid systems

          
Landers, Flamingo Heights and the homesteads have ground mounts and roof arrays charging batteries. Ground mounts need no roof access at all.

        

        

          
View home arrays

          
Joshua Tree and Pioneertown homes with bigger systems on a low roof. A 36 panel array is $252 and stands on its own, and the glass below it is usually the other reason we are there.

        Birds
        
What else we look for

      

      

        
Pigeons like a solar array in the basin for the same reasons they like one in Hesperia: shade, dry, out of the wind. If we find them during a wash you get a firm price on the spot. Pigeon proofing is $450 for up to 12 panels with the cleanout, clipped steel mesh, 2 to 3 spinners and the panel wash included, and it meets the basin minimum on its own.

        FAQ
        
Yucca Valley and Joshua Tree solar cleaning questions

      

      

        

          How much is solar panel cleaning in Yucca Valley or Joshua Tree?
          
$7 a panel with purified water, no trip fee. A 20 panel array is $140, a 36 panel array is $252. Visits this far out have a $249 minimum, so a smaller array pairs with a window cleaning or screens on the same stop.

        

        

          Do you clean off grid arrays and ground mounts?
          
Yes. Ground mounts need no roof access, and roof arrays with batteries behind them get the same purified water wash. We do not touch the electrical side; panels, frames and the glass are what we clean.

        

        

          How often should basin panels be washed?
          
Twice a year keeps up with most roofs here, with the spring wind season the time it matters most. Rentals that sit under a lot of dust do better every 3 months, at 25% off each visit on the plan.

        

        

          Is my requested day confirmed when I send the quote?
          
Not yet, it is a request. Solar washes out here ride on the same route days as other basin work, so Tony texts you the date and a morning or afternoon window, and the visit is set only once he confirms it with you.

        

        

          Have you cleaned solar in Yucca Valley or Joshua Tree before?
          
Not yet. The solar photos on this page come from our High Desert jobs, each labeled with its real town. The first Yucca Valley and Joshua Tree arrays get the same purified water wash, the same $7 a panel and the same written terms as every other customer, and their photos replace these once the homeowners say yes.
