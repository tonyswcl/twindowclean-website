Get a number faster

What to put in the text

You do not need any of this to call. It just gets you a firmer number faster.

Windows

City, one or two stories, roughly how many windows, and whether you want inside too. A photo of the front of the house is plenty.

Solar and pigeons

Panel count if you know it, one or two stories, and a photo from the ground showing the array edge. If you can see nesting or droppings, say so.

Screens

How many, and how many are sliding doors. A photo of the worst one tells us if the frame is reusable.

Storefront or graffiti

Business name, street, a photo of the glass and whether it is painted, sticker or etched. Say if you need it before opening.

The process

What happens after you reach out

  • 1⃣ You get a starting number the same day From the photo and your answers. It is the number on the website adjusted for what you actually have, and it is close.
  • 2⃣ We come look, free Ten minutes for a house, a bit longer for a roof. We count, check access, and show you anything you should know about before you decide.
  • 3⃣ Firm quote in writing Text or email. That number is the bill unless you add work on the day.
  • 4⃣ The job, then photos Done when we said. Before and after photos on request. Paid by a QuickBooks payment link or Zelle.

        FAQ
        
Contact questions

      


      

        

          What is the fastest way to get a price?
          
Text a photo to 714-559-0300 with your city and whether the house is one or two stories. For solar, include the panel count if you know it. For screens, how many and whether any are sliding doors. You will usually have a starting number back within the hour.

        

        

          Do I need to be home for the quote?
          
For exterior work, no. We can look at the outside of the house or the storefront and text you the number. For interior glass, pigeon proofing under panels, or anything on the roof, we like you there so we can show you what we found.

        

        

          Do you reply on weekends?
          
Yes. Texts and form submissions get read seven days a week. Weekend jobs are booked for commercial accounts that cannot close during the week and for anything urgent like graffiti.

        

        

          Can I book online?
          
Send the form or text and we set a time together. We do not use an automatic calendar because most jobs need a two minute conversation first about access, water and what is actually on the glass. It saves everyone a wasted visit.

        

        

          Do you work with property managers and multiple sites?
          
Yes. Several sites on one schedule and one invoice a month. Certificate of insurance on file before the first visit. See the commercial page for how recurring accounts work.
