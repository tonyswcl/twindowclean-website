1. About the Company Hub

The Company Hub ("the app") is a private business app that Tony's Window Cleaning ("we", "the company") built for its own use. It keeps the company's jobs, schedule and payment requests, and it can connect to the company's own QuickBooks Online account. The app is not sold, offered or licensed to any other business or person.

2. Who may use it

Only the company's owner and the staff the owner gives an account may use the app. This license is personal to the company, cannot be transferred, and ends when the owner removes an account or stops using the app.

3. QuickBooks Online

  • The app connects to QuickBooks Online only after the owner signs in to QuickBooks and approves the connection.
  • It reads payments, sales receipts, refund receipts, invoices and deposits so the owner can match them to jobs.
  • It creates an invoice for a payment request only when the owner reviews that request in the app and taps Create. The app does not email or send anything to a customer from QuickBooks.
  • It does not create expenses, bills, vendors or other accounting entries, and it does not change, delete or void existing QuickBooks records.
  • QuickBooks and QuickBooks Online are trademarks of Intuit Inc. Intuit is not a party to this agreement. Use of QuickBooks is also governed by Intuit's own terms.

4. Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect the app at any time with Disconnect QuickBooks in the app, or in QuickBooks Online (Settings, then Apps). After disconnecting, the app no longer reads from or writes to QuickBooks. Invoices it already created stay in QuickBooks unchanged. Information the app already holds is handled as described in the privacy policy.
