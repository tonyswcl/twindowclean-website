Company Hub Privacy Policy

Tony's Window Cleaning, Hesperia, California · Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy covers the Company Hub ("the app"), the private business app of Tony's Window Cleaning ("we"), and its connection to our QuickBooks Online account. Our public website has its own privacy policy.

What the app holds

  • Our own job records: names and contact details customers give us for service, service addresses, schedules, prices and payments.
  • When QuickBooks Online is connected: our QuickBooks company id, the connection token, and copies of the QuickBooks records the app reads to match money to jobs. These are payments, sales receipts, refund receipts, invoices and deposits, including amounts, dates and the customer they belong to.
  • For each payment request the app creates in QuickBooks: the invoice number, amount and payment link.

What the QuickBooks connection does today

  • It connects only after our owner signs in to QuickBooks and approves it, and it asks Intuit for QuickBooks Online Accounting access only.
  • It reads the record types listed above when QuickBooks tells the app they changed.
  • It creates an invoice only when our owner reviews a payment request in the app and taps Create. It does not email customers from QuickBooks.
  • It does not create expenses, bills, vendors or other accounting entries, and it does not change, delete or void existing QuickBooks records.
  • If we add other QuickBooks features, we will update this policy before they are used.

How it is used

Only to run our business: scheduling, payment requests, matching payments to jobs and keeping our books accurate. We do not sell or rent this information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Who can see it

Everyone who uses the app signs in with their own account. QuickBooks data in the app is available only to our owner. Staff accounts do not see QuickBooks data, money settings or profit figures. These limits are set in the database, not only in the screens.

Service providers and where information is processed

  • Supabase runs the app's database, sign in and server functions. The database is in Supabase's Canada (Central) region. Server functions run on Supabase's network and may process requests outside Canada, including in the United States.
  • Netlify serves the app's pages. It does not store app records.
  • Intuit provides QuickBooks Online, under Intuit's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google may receive job schedules when our Google Calendar is connected, and alert emails to our owner. The QuickBooks connection does not send QuickBooks records to Google.
  • Anthropic: our owner uses Claude, an AI assistant, to help keep our records. It may read app records when our owner asks it to.

Because of these providers, information may be processed or accessed in Canada, the United States and other countries where they operate.

How it is protected

  • Sign in is required, and database access depends on each person's role.
  • The QuickBooks connection token is stored encrypted in the database vault. Only the app's server functions read it, and it is not sent to phones or browsers.
  • The app's pages and data connections use HTTPS.

We take reasonable care to protect this information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

  • Our owner can disconnect at any time with Disconnect QuickBooks in the app, or in QuickBooks Online (Settings, then Apps).
  • Disconnecting in the app asks Intuit to revoke the app's access and deletes the stored connection token. When access is ended in QuickBooks instead, the app deletes the stored token the next time it tries to use it and Intuit refuses.
  • After disconnecting, the app can no longer read from or write to QuickBooks. Records and invoices inside QuickBooks are not affected.
  • Information the app already holds, such as copied QuickBooks records, invoice numbers, payment links and payments recorded on jobs, is not deleted automatically. It is handled under our business record retention and deletion procedures.

Keeping information and deletion requests

  • We keep job and payment records, including necessary financial history, as long as they are needed for bookkeeping, tax compliance, audit trails and our legal obligations.
  • A customer may ask what we hold about them, or ask us to correct or delete eligible information, by contacting us below. We will respond, subject to the record keeping requirements above.
