        FAQ
        
Questions, answered

      


      

        

          What services do you offer?
          
Window cleaning inside and out, window screen repair, solar panel cleaning and pigeon proofing, plus commercial window cleaning, hard water stain removal, and graffiti and adhesive removal. Pressure washing, whole roof soft washing (from $599), gutter cleaning and trash bin cleaning are custom quotes after we see the job. Need more than one? It all goes on one quote.

        

        

          How much is window screen repair?
Standard Re-Mesh starts at $53.99 with a usable existing frame. All-Weather Upgrade starts at $64.99 with a usable frame. Full Upgrade starts at $84.99 with All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh and a new custom frame. The screen-only minimum is $149 and discounts do not reduce it. Oversize screens, sliding doors, specialty fabrication and extra hardware are assessed separately.

        

          Do you offer pigeon proofing or pigeon removal?
          
Yes. We clean out the nests and droppings under your solar panels, clip mesh to the panel frame with no drilling, and add 2 to 3 reflective spinners, so pigeons have nowhere to nest under the array. We serve Hesperia, Victorville, Apple Valley, Adelanto and the rest of the High Desert.

        

        

          How much does pigeon proofing cost?
          
Pigeon proofing starts at $450 for up to 12 panels on a single story home. A two story home adds $125, and each panel past 12 adds $50. That includes removing old nests and mesh, full dropping cleanup and sanitizing, new mesh secured with clips, a solar wash and 2 to 3 reflective spinners. The inspection is free and the quote is firm before we start. See what's included →

        

        

          How much do you charge to clean solar panels?
          
Solar panel cleaning uses purified water and a soft brush. The existing $7 per-panel rate applies to eligible work, with access, minimums and the final scope confirmed in your quote. Cleaning is separate from electrical inspection and does not promise a specific production increase.

        

        

          Do you offer a warranty on pigeon proofing?
          
Yes. Our pigeon proofing carries a lifetime workmanship warranty. We secure the mesh with clips rather than drilling bolts through it, because bolt-mounted mesh tears loose in High Desert wind. It is for the original customer and does not transfer if the property is sold. If mesh or clips fail because of how we installed them, we repair or replace the affected mesh and clips at no charge. It does not cover storm damage or birds getting in through areas that were not originally protected, and removal or changes by anyone else void it for the whole installation.

        

        

          Do you clean solar panels?
          
Yes. Purified water runs through a soft brush on a pole, so the panels are cleaned without soap or pressure, dry without spots, and nobody walks on them. If we notice anything visible while we are up there, like a cracked panel or birds, we point it out.

        

        

          What areas do you serve?
          
Our regular area is Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley, Adelanto, Oak Hills, Phelan, Fontana, Helendale, Silver Lakes and Baldy Mesa. By request, on scheduled route days, we also come to Barstow, Lucerne Valley, Piñon Hills, Wrightwood, the Cajon Pass, Crestline, Lake Arrowhead, Running Springs, Big Bear Lake, and in the Inland Empire Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Upland, Chino Hills, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Corona, Norco, Rialto, San Bernardino, Highland, Redlands, Yucaipa and San Jacinto. Not sure if you're in range? Just reach out and we'll let you know.

        

        

          Do you do commercial jobs?
          
Absolutely. From storefronts to offices and recurring service, contact us for a custom quote tailored to your property.

        

        

          Can I book just one service?
          
Yes. Window cleaning, mobile screen repair and replacement, solar panel cleaning and pigeon-proofing can each be quoted separately. Choose the relevant service page or select the work you need in the service request form. Tony confirms scope, access and scheduling before booking.

        

        

          How do I book?
          
Use the service request form, or call/text 714-559-0300. You can also message us on Facebook or Yelp.
