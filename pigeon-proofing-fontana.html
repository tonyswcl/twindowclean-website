        Neighborhoods
        
Where the Fontana calls come from

      

      

        

          
North Fontana

          
Sierra Lakes, Coyote Canyon, Summit Heights and Hunter's Ridge. Newer two story tracts with large arrays, and HOAs that care what the roof looks like from the street. Low profile clipped mesh is built for exactly that.

        

        

          
Southridge

          
Established single story homes with retrofit arrays, often split across two roof planes. Two roof faces means the second free spinner earns its keep.

        

        

          
Jurupa Hills and south of the 10

          
Older tile roofs where the birds have often been under the panels for years. The cleanout is bigger here and the price is the same.

        

      

    

  


  

    

      

        HOA
        
What the mesh looks like from the street

      

      

        
Fontana HOAs ask two things: no visible changes to the roof line and nothing that looks like chicken wire. Our mesh is dark, rigid steel that sits inside the shadow line of the panels and is clipped to the frame lip rather than screwed, so there are no fasteners, no brackets and no holes in the roof. From the sidewalk the array looks the way it did the day it was installed, minus the birds.

        FAQ
        
Fontana pigeon proofing questions

      

      

        

          Do you really come to Fontana?
          
Yes. Fontana is one of our regular Inland Empire stops, grouped with Rancho Cucamonga, Rialto and Ontario. No trip fee and the same prices as the High Desert.

        

        

          What does it cost in Fontana?
          
$450 for up to 12 panels and $50 for each panel over, with the cleanout, mesh, 2 to 3 spinners, a panel wash and cleanup of the droppings under and around the panels included. A 20 panel array is $850.

        

        

          Will my HOA have a problem with the mesh?
          
We have not had one. The mesh is dark steel that sits inside the shadow line of the panels, clipped to the frame with no brackets, screws or roof penetrations. From the street the roof line does not change.

        

        

          How soon can you get here?
          
Usually within the week, on a day we are already down the hill. Send your quote with a morning or afternoon preference and Tony texts back the day.

        

        

          Do you clean the panels too?
          
Yes, a purified water wash of the whole array and cleanup of the droppings under and around the panels are included with pigeon proofing. On their own those would be $7 a panel and from $599.
