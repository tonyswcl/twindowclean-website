        Wind
        
What it does to glass and screens

      

      

        
Wind blown sand does two things: it coats the glass in a film that smears when it gets wet, and over years it frosts the surface of west facing panes so they never look fully clear again. We scrub with purified water so the grit lifts instead of grinding, and we tell you honestly when a pane has been sandblasted past what a cleaning can fix.

        
For sun-facing windows, All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh is an option from $64.99 using a usable frame. Full Upgrade with a new custom frame starts at $84.99. We assess the opening and confirm suitable materials before work.

        FAQ
        
Cajon Pass questions

      

      

        

          Do you come to Lytle Creek and Devore?
          
Yes, on our Fontana and Rialto days, and to Cajon Junction and Summit Valley on the way down. No trip fee anywhere in the pass.

        

        

          What does it cost up the mountain?
          
The same as the High Desert: windows from $149 single story and $249 two story with screens, tracks and sills included, screens re-meshed from $53.99, solar $7 a panel. The only rule for mountain visits is a $149 job minimum. No trip fee.

        

        

          My west windows look hazy even when clean. Is that fixable?
          
Sometimes. Wind blown sand frosts the surface of glass over years, and no cleaning reverses that. A purified water scrub gets everything off that will come off, and we tell you before we start which panes are etched.

        

        

          What screens hold up in the pass?
Standard Re-Mesh starts at $53.99 with a usable existing frame. All-Weather Upgrade starts at $64.99 with a usable frame. Full Upgrade starts at $84.99 with All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh and a new custom frame. The screen-only minimum is $149 and discounts do not reduce it. Oversize screens, sliding doors, specialty fabrication and extra hardware are assessed separately. Screen-only work uses two appointments: pickup and inspection, then a separate confirmed return for drop-off, installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. You can request a return date or let Tony confirm it after inspection. Requested dates are not confirmed bookings; pickup is not completed work. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for the job. Solar-control mesh provides additional shading, with changes to outward visibility, airflow and incoming light.

        

          How often should pass homes be cleaned?
          
Every 3 months on the plan, which is 25% off each visit after the first. It is the only schedule that keeps the sand from bonding to the glass.
