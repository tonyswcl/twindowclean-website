How Often to Clean Solar Panels Up Here

Every 3 months for most High Desert roofs. On a plan that is 25% off each visit after the first, and we text you before every one.

Down the hill, rain rinses panels a few times a winter. Up here we get a handful of storms a year and wind most weeks, so dust builds until someone washes it off. The question is not whether to clean, it is how often, and the honest answer depends on your street.

How often

A schedule by where you live

Every 3 months

Open desert edges of Hesperia, Adelanto, Phelan and Oak Hills, anything next to a dirt road, and any array near sprinklers. Dust and overspray both bake on in a summer.

Twice a year

Established tracts in Victorville, Apple Valley and Spring Valley Lake with paved streets and lawns. Spring after the wind season, fall after the summer heat.

Once a year, at least

Shaded or sheltered arrays. Even these film over by the end of a dry year, and once a year is when we catch birds before they have raised a second brood.

What dust does

Why the schedule matters

Dust and sprinkler overspray settle into a film that the little rain we get does not move, and summer heat bakes it on. Your solar app is the honest way to judge what it does on your roof: compare a clear day right after a wash with one a couple of months later. We do not promise a number, because it depends on the season, the street and the array. Droppings are a different story: they bake onto the glass and cover cells outright.

The 3 month visit is also when things get caught early. If we notice anything visible, like a cracked panel or birds at the array edge, we point it out. If you have pigeon mesh, the clips and mesh get looked over and the spinners get a spin. Nothing on a roof fixes itself.

The plan

What a plan is and what it is not

A plan is a schedule, not a contract. Every 3 months is 25% off each visit after the first, twice a year is 15% off, once a year is the regular price with a reminder. Tony texts you before every visit, you can skip one, and there is nothing to cancel.

At $7 a panel, a 20 panel array is $140 on the first visit and $105 on each plan visit after that. Four visits a year run about $455 instead of $560 booked one at a time. Include your panel count and ask about a quarterly plan in your service request.

With pigeon proofing

If you have mesh or want it

Pigeon proofing includes the panel wash free on the day, and a year of cleanings can be added: three more washes, every 3 months, at $7 a panel. Each one doubles as a spinner, clip and mesh check, which is exactly when a loose clip gets caught before a bird does.

        FAQ
        
Solar cleaning schedule questions

      

      

        

          How often should solar panels be cleaned in the High Desert?
          
Every 3 months for arrays near open desert, dirt roads or sprinklers, twice a year for established tracts with paved streets, and at least once a year for anything sheltered. There is not enough rain up here to count on it.

        

        

          Does rain clean solar panels?
          
A hard rain rinses loose dust, but it does not move baked on film or droppings, and it leaves its own mineral spots as it dries. Up here we get so few storms that rain is not a cleaning plan.

        

        

          How much output does dust really cost?
          
It depends on the season, the street and the array, so we do not promise a number. Your solar app will show it: compare a clear day right after a wash with one a couple of months later. Droppings matter more than dust, because they bake on and cover cells outright.

        

        

          What does a plan cost?
          
$7 a panel on the first visit. Every 3 months is 25% off each visit after that, twice a year is 15% off, once a year is the regular price. No contract, and Tony texts before every visit.

        

        

          Can I skip a visit?
          
Yes. The plan is a reminder and a discount, not a commitment. Reply to the text and we move it.
