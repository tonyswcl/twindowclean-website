What Pigeon Proofing Solar Panels Costs

$450 covers up to 12 panels. $50 a panel after that. Everything else is included, and here is the math.

Pigeon proofing quotes range from a few hundred dollars to well over a thousand, and the difference is usually what is left out. Ours includes the cleanout, the sanitizing, the mesh, the spinners, a panel wash and cleanup of the droppings under and around the panels. Below is what that adds up to for real array sizes.

The math

Three common arrays, priced

12 panels: $450

The base price covers the whole job on a typical single story array. Cleanout, mesh, 2 spinners, panel wash and cleanup of the droppings under and around the panels, all in.

20 panels: $850

$450 plus 8 panels over 12 at $50 each. The larger arrays in Hesperia's Ranchero tracts and Apple Valley land here, with 3 spinners free.

30 panels: $1,350

$450 plus 18 panels at $50. Two story homes with two roof planes usually need an extra spinner or two at $50 each so both sides are covered.

What is inside the price

Why the base price is $450

The cleanout is the biggest part of the labor. Nests, droppings and debris come out from under every panel and get hauled off, and the area is sanitized so the smell goes with it. A roof with a year of nesting can take half a day before any mesh goes on.

The mesh is rigid, rust resistant steel, cut to the array and held by clips that grip the lip of the panel frame. Nothing is drilled or screwed, so your panel warranty stays intact. Then the panels get washed with purified water, which on its own would be $7 a panel, and the roof under and around the panels gets a soft wash where the birds were.

What moves the price

The three things that change a quote

Panel count is the main one, at $50 a panel past 12. Roof shape is the second: an array split across the front and back of a two story usually wants an extra spinner so both sides get the flash. The third is how long the birds have been there, which changes the cleanout time but not the price. We quote the number and stick to it.

What does not change it: tile versus shingle, one story versus two, or how many birds are on the roof today.

Compared with waiting

What a year of not doing it costs

Droppings are acidic. A year on concrete tile leaves staining that does not fully come back, and a year on the panels bakes on over whole cells. Pigeons raise more than one brood a season, so the flock on your roof next spring is bigger than the one there now, and the cleanout with it.

        FAQ
        
Pigeon proofing cost questions

      

      

        

          How much does pigeon proofing cost for solar panels?
          
$450 for up to 12 panels, then $50 for each panel over 12. That includes the cleanout, sanitizing, rigid mesh clipped to the frame, 2 to 3 spinners, a panel wash and cleanup of the droppings beneath and around the panels. A 20 panel array is $850, a 30 panel array is $1,350.

        

        

          Why do some quotes come in lower?
          
Quotes differ mostly because the scope differs. Ask any company the same three questions: what happens to the nests and droppings, what the mesh is made of, and how it is attached. Ours includes the cleanout and sanitizing, rigid steel mesh, and clips on the panel frame with nothing drilled, all written into the quote.

        

        

          Are the spinners really free?
          
Two to three are included depending on the size of the roof. Extras are $50 each. A two story with panels on both sides usually wants one more so the flash reaches the back.

        

        

          Is the price firm?
          
Yes. The price Tony confirms in writing after reviewing the property is the agreed price. Nothing gets added on the day, including if the cleanout takes longer than expected.

        

        

          Is there a warranty?
          
Lifetime workmanship warranty, in writing. It is for the original customer and does not transfer if the property is sold. If mesh or clips fail because of how we installed them, we repair or replace the affected mesh and clips at no charge. It does not cover storm damage or birds getting in through areas that were not originally protected, and removal or changes by anyone else void it for the whole installation.
