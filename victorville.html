        Victorville
        
Victorville is really six different jobs

      

      

        
Victorville is the biggest city we cover and the least consistent. A house on the lake and a house off D Street have almost nothing in common, so we quote them nothing alike.

        
Spring Valley Lake is its own problem. Lake-fed irrigation is hard, and years of sprinkler overspray leaves mineral etching in the glass that a normal wash simply will not touch. Waterfowl and pigeons both work that side of town, and the pigeons move onto the roofs.

        
The newer tracts off Bear Valley went up with large solar arrays as standard, and a lot of them are two story. That combination is the single most common pigeon proofing call we get out of this city.

        
Then there is Old Town, where the housing stock is decades older, the windows are original, and nobody has touched the screens or the tracks since the place was built. Different work entirely, and priced that way.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Neighborhoods
        
Victorville neighborhoods we work in

        
Different parts of this city need genuinely different things. Here is what we usually find where.

      

      

        

          
Spring Valley Lake

          
Hard water etching from lake irrigation is the headline here, plus birds working the waterfront. Most of our glass restoration work in the city is on these streets. The lake now has its own page.

        

        

          
Eagle Ranch

          
Newer master-planned homes with large arrays. High pigeon pressure and a lot of two story roofs.

        

        

          
Brentwood

          
Established tract housing, moderate arrays, and gutters that need clearing before they start holding nests.

        

        

          
Old Town Victorville

          
Original single-pane windows, aluminum screens well past their life, and tracks nobody has ever detailed.

        

        

          
Mojave Heights

          
Exposed and dusty. Panels film over quickly and screens take a beating from blown grit.

        

        

          
Green Tree and the Golden Triangle

          
Mature neighborhoods with heavy tree cover, which means gutters, roof debris and the nesting that follows.

        

        

          
Mountain View Acres

          
Bigger parcels on the west side. Long driveways and outbuildings that often need pressure washing at the same time.

        FAQ
        
Victorville questions

      

      

        

          Why do my windows look cloudy even right after a wash?
          
That is almost certainly hard water etching, not dirt. It is very common at Spring Valley Lake where the irrigation is mineral heavy. A normal wash will not lift it, it takes a restoration process. We will do one pane and show you the difference before you commit to the whole house.

        

        

          Do you do two story homes in Eagle Ranch?
          
Yes, and often. Two story is standard for us, not a special request. Exterior window cleaning on a two story starts at $249 and roof work is quoted after we look at the pitch and the access.

        

        

          How much is pigeon proofing in Victorville?
          
From $450. The Bear Valley and Eagle Ranch tracts tend to have larger arrays, so those run higher, usually $600 to $900. Free inspection and a firm written number first.

        

        

          Can you clean the solar on a two story in Spring Valley Lake?
          
Yes. Water fed pole reaches most two story arrays from the ground, which is safer and faster than putting anyone on a wet roof. If yours needs roof access we will say so on the inspection.
