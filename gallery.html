Gallery questions

      


      

        

          Are any of these stock photos?
          
No. Every image on this site was taken on one of our jobs, by us, in the city named in the caption. If a roof or a street looks familiar, it probably is.

        

        

          Can I see photos from my city?
          
Yes. Each city page carries only photos from that city, and each service page carries the best examples of that service. This page pulls from all of them.

        

        

          Do you photograph every job?
          
Yes, before and after. Commercial customers get them in a written report. Residential customers get them on request. We ask before posting any exterior of a home and never show house numbers.

        

        

          Are these your own photos?
          
Yes. Every photo is from our own jobs in the High Desert. No stock photos.
