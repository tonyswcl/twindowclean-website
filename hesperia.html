        Hesperia
        
What the Cajon Pass does to a Hesperia house

      

      

        
This is home base. Our truck is somewhere in Hesperia most days of the week, which is the only reason we can usually get someone out here the same week you call instead of the week after.

        
The wind out of the Cajon Pass is what makes this town different from anywhere else we work. It comes up the grade loaded with fine grit and drives it sideways into everything. Window tracks pack solid, screen mesh goes chalky and brittle, and solar panels build a film that our four inches of annual rain will never take off.

        
Older screens may have brittle mesh or worn frames. We inspect them before recommending new mesh or a replacement frame. Screen-only jobs use pickup and inspection, followed by a separate confirmed return for installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. Both dates are requests until Tony confirms them. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for your job.

        
Pigeons are worst on anything with solar between Ranchero and Main. The gap under a panel is shaded, warm and out of that pass wind, which is exactly what a bird wants when there is nothing but open desert for miles in every direction.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Neighborhoods
        
Hesperia neighborhoods we work in

        
We cover the whole city, but these are the pockets we are in most often and what tends to come up in each.

      

      

        

          
The Mesa

          
Larger lots and dirt frontage roads mean more airborne grit than the rest of town. Screens and tracks take the worst of it here.

        

        

          
Ranchero Road corridor

          
Newer tract homes, big south-facing arrays, and the highest concentration of pigeon calls we get in Hesperia.

        

        

          
Hesperia Golf & Country Club

          
Sprinkler overspray off the course side etches hard water into glass. Most of our hard water restoration work in town is on these streets.

        

        

          
Downtown Hesperia

          
Older ranch-style homes with original aluminum-frame screens, red tile roofs, and windows nobody has professionally cleaned in years.

        

        

          
Summit Valley

          
Exposed and windy, further out. We batch these with other Summit Valley stops, and there is no trip charge.

        

        

          
Mesa Linda

          
Established streets, mature trees, and gutters that fill with needles and then attract nesting.

        FAQ
        
Hesperia questions

      

      

        

          Are you actually based in Hesperia?
          
Yes. Hesperia is home base, not a service area we drive out to. That is why we can usually fit you in the same week rather than pushing you two weeks out.

        

        

          Why do my window tracks fill with sand so fast?
          
The Cajon Pass funnels wind straight into town carrying fine grit. It gets past weatherstripping and settles in the track. We vacuum and detail the tracks as part of every exterior window clean here, it is not an upcharge.

        

        

          What does pigeon proofing cost in Hesperia?
          
From $450, which covers clearing the nests and droppings, hauling them off, sealing the full perimeter with mesh clipped to the panel frame, and a lifetime workmanship warranty. Plus 2 to 3 reflective spinners, installed free. Larger Ranchero-area arrays run higher. The inspection is free.

        

        

          Can you get to Summit Valley or is that too far out?
          
We go, we just group Summit Valley stops together so one customer is not carrying the drive. If you are out that way tell us when you call and we will tell you honestly which day we are already headed there.
