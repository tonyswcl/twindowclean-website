        Snow and screens
        
What a winter does to a cabin

      

      

        
Snow sliding off a metal roof takes screens with it, and what it leaves bows out of the spline by spring. Fiberglass mesh that has gone through a few freeze thaw seasons tears at a touch. All weather re-meshing is $64.99 a screen, with Full Upgrade from $84.99 when needed. Screen-only jobs use pickup and inspection, followed by a separate confirmed return for drop-off, installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for your job.

        
Inside, a season of the wood stove leaves a film on every pane. The $49 inside add on clears it and vacuums the tracks, which fill with needles and grit.

        FAQ
        
Running Springs questions

      

      

        

          Can you reach windows over a steep drop?
          
Yes. Deck side glass is cleaned from the deck with a water fed pole, and gable glass from the ground or the deck below it. Nobody stands on a slope they should not be on.

        

        

          What does it cost up the mountain?
          
The same as the High Desert: windows from $149 single story and $249 two story with screens, tracks and sills included, screens re-meshed from $53.99, solar $7 a panel. The only rule for mountain visits is a $149 job minimum. No trip fee.

        

        

          My screens are torn every spring. What do you suggest?
Standard Re-Mesh starts at $53.99 with a usable existing frame. All-Weather Upgrade starts at $64.99 with a usable frame. Full Upgrade starts at $84.99 with All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh and a new custom frame. The screen-only minimum is $149 and discounts do not reduce it. Oversize screens, sliding doors, specialty fabrication and extra hardware are assessed separately. Screen-only work uses two appointments: pickup and inspection, then a separate confirmed return for drop-off, installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. You can request a return date or let Tony confirm it after inspection. Requested dates are not confirmed bookings; pickup is not completed work. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for the job. Solar-control mesh provides additional shading, with changes to outward visibility, airflow and incoming light.

        

          Do you come up in winter?
          
Yes, when the roads are clear. We schedule around storms and chain controls, and if a day turns we text and move it rather than show up late to a snowed in driveway.

        

        

          Can you get pine sap and pollen off the glass?
          
Yes. Pollen film rinses off with purified water and a scrub. Sap takes a solvent and a little patience, pane by pane, and it comes off without scratching when the glass is worked wet.
