What is different about Phelan

      

      

        
Every Phelan property we service draws hard water, whether from Sheep Creek or a private well. Left to dry on glass it leaves mineral spotting that nothing in a spray bottle removes, so we wash and rinse with deionized water throughout.

        
At 4,100 feet the temperature swing also drives dust into window frames and tracks harder than it does on the valley floor. We detail the tracks on every exterior clean rather than treating it as an extra.

        
No trip charge anywhere in Phelan, and no charge to come out and look.

        FAQ
        
Window Cleaning questions

      

      

        

          Do you cover Phelan?
          
Yes. We come up to Phelan and Piñon Hills on grouped days so the drive is shared. We work across Phelan Village Center, Baldy Mesa West, the Sheep Creek corridor and Piñon Hills. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

        

        

          What does window cleaning cost in Phelan?
          
From $149 single story and $249 two story with screens, tracks and sills included. No trip charge for Phelan, we just group the days.

        

        

          Why does my glass spot so badly up here?
          
Well water in this area is hard, and when it dries on glass the minerals stay behind. We wash and rinse with purified deionized water so there is nothing left to dry into a spot.

        

        

          Why does the glass look cloudy after a normal wash?
          
That is usually hard water etched into the glass by sprinkler overspray, not dirt. It needs a restoration process rather than a wash. We will tell you honestly if the etching has gone too deep to fully recover.
