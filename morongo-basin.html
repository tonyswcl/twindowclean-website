The towns

Where the route days go

Yucca Valley

The largest town in the basin, where Highway 247 meets Highway 62. Ranch homes on big lots, newer tracts toward Yucca Mesa, and solar on many of the newer roofs. Grouped with the rest of the basin on one day.

Joshua Tree

Homesteads and modern desert houses built around glass, many of them short term rentals. Big panes, a lot of dust, and owners who are not on site.

Twentynine Palms

Homes near the base and along Adobe Road, farther east and drier still. The longest leg of a basin day, so it pairs best with Joshua Tree stops.

Morongo Valley and Landers

Morongo Valley on the way in from the Pass side, Landers and Flamingo Heights up Old Woman Springs Road on the way in from ours. Off grid arrays in Landers are solar work with no grid to fall back on.

Desert glass and panels

What the basin does to a home

Dust that never rinses off

The basin gets even less rain than the High Desert, and the wind runs every afternoon in spring. Glass and panels carry a film a storm never clears, so a cleaning holds until the next blow, not the next rain.

Big glass

Modern desert homes here are built for the view, with sliders and fixed panes taller than a person. That is water fed pole work: purified water up a carbon pole, scrubbed and rinsed from the ground, dried spot free with no ladder against the glass.

Rentals between guests

Give us the checkout and check in times and the job goes in that window, with a lockbox code and photos texted when it is done. Outside glass, screens and tracks in one visit, inside glass $49 more when the dust has come in.

Screens and the wind

Afternoon wind and sun wear fiberglass mesh out fast here. We re-mesh at the truck, All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh at $64.99 a screen, and screens added to a window cleaning help a visit reach the $249 minimum.

Prices and the minimum

The same numbers, one rule

Windows $149 single story and $249 two story outside, screens, tracks and sills included, inside every window $49 more. Solar $7 a panel with purified water. Pigeon proofing $450 for up to 12 panels, with the cleanout, clipped mesh, 2 to 3 spinners and a panel wash included. Screens re-meshed from $53.99, all weather $64.99. Pressure washing, hard water removal, gutters and the rest are in the quote under Find our other services, priced on site.

The one Morongo Basin rule is a $249 job minimum per visit. Pigeon proofing meets it alone, so does a two story window cleaning, and a single story window cleaning usually pairs with a solar wash or screens to get there. Second homes and rentals

Work you do not have to stand over

A lot of basin homes are second homes and short term rentals, so the work often happens with nobody watching. That is where a written scope earns its keep: you know before the visit what is included, outside work gets done the same whether or not anyone is home, and photos come by text when it is finished if you want them. If a window has hard water etched in or an array has birds under it, Tony sends the price first instead of adding it on.

How a route day works

From the quote to a date

The day you pick in the quote is a preference. Basin requests are collected and grouped so one drive covers several homes, and Tony then texts from 714-559-0300 with the date and whether it is a morning or an afternoon. The confirmed date is the booking, and payment comes after the work, once you have looked it over.

        FAQ
        
Morongo Basin questions

      

      

        

          Do you come to Yucca Valley and Joshua Tree?
          
Yes, by request on grouped route days, about an hour and a quarter from Hesperia down Highway 247. Yucca Valley, Joshua Tree, Twentynine Palms, Morongo Valley, Landers and Pioneertown. Same prices, no trip fee, a $249 job minimum per visit.

        

        

          Is there a trip fee or a minimum?
          
There is no trip fee, and the prices are the ones on every page here. What the basin asks for is a $249 job minimum per visit. Pigeon proofing or a two story window cleaning meets it alone, and smaller jobs reach it by pairing two services. Tony reads every basin request before anything goes on the calendar.

Yes. Tell us the checkout and check in times and the job is scheduled inside that window on a basin route day, with a lockbox code and photos texted when it is done. Managers with several homes get one schedule and one invoice.
