What Window Cleaning Costs in the High Desert

Tony's Window Cleaning, Hesperia · November 20, 2026

Here, exterior window cleaning starts at $149 for an eligible single story home and $249 for a two story home, with the screens, tracks and sills included. What moves a quote from there is how many windows you have, how high they are, whether you want the inside done, and whether there's hard water etched into the glass.

What the starting price includes

  • Every exterior window on the house.
  • Screens taken off and cleaned.
  • Tracks and sills hand-detailed.
  • A purified, deionized water finish that dries clear, with no soap film left for the next round of dust to stick to.

Screens, tracks and sills are in the number, not billed on top.

What changes the price

Height. A two story home means taller ladders, more setup, more time and more risk. That's the difference between $149 and $249.

Window count. The starting prices cover a typical home. More than 12 windows on a single story, or more than 20 on a two story, adds a flat $39.

Inside glass. Optional. The inside of every window in the house is a flat $49 on the same visit.

Hard water. Sprinkler overspray leaves minerals that etch into glass, and a normal wash doesn't remove them. Restoration is a separate process from $12 a pane. We clean one test pane free first so you can see whether it's worth doing.

Special glass. French doors and unusually high glass are quoted when we see them.

Screens that need repair. Cleaning is included. Torn mesh or a bent frame is a separate repair, from $53.99 a screen to re-mesh.

Per window or per house?

Many national price guides quote by the window or by the pane, so the total is hard to know until someone counts. We start from a price for the house and list the add-ons, so you know the number before we start. Your written quote shows exactly what's included.

How often to clean

There's no fixed schedule that suits every home. Windows that face the wind or the sprinklers show it first. Look at those after a windy stretch, and clean when they bother you.

What you may not need

  • Inside cleaning. If the inside glass looks fine, skip it. It's an option, not part of the base price.
  • Hard water restoration. Many spots are surface deposits that come off in a normal clean. Restoration only makes sense if the free test pane shows etching, and we'll tell you honestly which one you have.
