A screen that fits, with an even finish
A good screen needs a frame that fits the opening and mesh held at an even tension. We check the frame, corners and retaining clips, then recommend re-meshing a sound frame or replacing a bent or missing frame. Choose charcoal fiberglass or the heavier vinyl-coated polyester all-weather material. We explain the view, airflow and daylight tradeoffs before quoting the work.
Do I need new mesh or a new frame?
A straight frame with sound corners may only need new mesh and spline. Bent, damaged or missing frames need a different scope. Your written quote identifies re-meshing and frame replacement separately.
Is all-weather mesh pet-proof, and is there a minimum?
All-weather mesh is a material option, not a promise that pets cannot damage it. Screen-only visits have the existing $149 minimum; discounts do not reduce that floor. Adding screen work to another service is priced in that quote.

        Replace or re-mesh
        
Which one your screen needs

      

      

        
Re-meshing keeps your frame and gives it new mesh. It costs $53.99 in charcoal fiberglass or $64.99 in all weather, and it is the right call when the frame is straight and the corners still hold. Most screens we see qualify.

        
Replacement is for the rest: frames that came off in the wind and got stepped on, corners that crumbled, screens that were never there when you bought the house. Standard Re-Mesh starts at $53.99 with a usable existing frame. All-Weather Upgrade starts at $64.99 with a usable frame. Full Upgrade starts at $84.99 with solar-control mesh and a new custom frame. The screen-only minimum is $149; oversize screens, doors, specialty fabrication and extra hardware are assessed separately.

        
We decide screen by screen at your window, not from the driveway. A house with 14 screens might need 11 re-meshed and 3 rebuilt, and that is exactly how it goes on the quote.

        Why they fail here
        
What High Desert screens go through

      

      

        
Builder mesh is fine at sea level. Up here it gets nine months of hard UV, then wind that runs 30 to 50 miles an hour a few times each season. The mesh goes brittle first, then a gust bows it out of the spline and the frame twists. Once a frame twists it never sits right again.

        
All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh is an option for sun-facing windows. The vinyl-coated polyester is durable and pet-resistant; its denser weave changes outward visibility, airflow and incoming light. We confirm suitability before quoting.

        FAQ
        
Window screen replacement questions

      

      

        

          Do I need a whole new screen or just new mesh?
          
If the frame is straight and the corners hold, new mesh is enough and it is $10 cheaper. If the frame is bent, missing corners or missing altogether, we build a new one. We check each screen at the window and the quote says which is which.

        

        

          How much is a new window screen?
Standard Re-Mesh starts at $53.99 with a usable existing frame. All-Weather Upgrade starts at $64.99 with a usable frame. Full Upgrade starts at $84.99 with All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh and a new custom frame. The screen-only minimum is $149 and discounts do not reduce it. Oversize screens, sliding doors, specialty fabrication and extra hardware are assessed separately.

        

          Can you match my existing screens?
          
Frames come in the standard finishes and the mesh is charcoal, the same as most builder screens, so a rebuilt screen sits next to an original without standing out. If your home has bronze frames we match those too.

        

        

          Do you do this at my house or take them away?
Screen-only work uses two appointments: pickup and inspection, then a separate confirmed return for drop-off, installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. You can request a return date or let Tony confirm it after inspection. Requested dates are not confirmed bookings; pickup is not completed work. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for the job.

        

          What is the warranty?
Your written quote states any applicable material and installation coverage, exclusions and claim process. Manufacturer material specifications are separate from installation coverage. Existing written customer agreements are unchanged.
