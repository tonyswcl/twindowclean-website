Where we work in Hesperia

        
The corridors, what is on the glass there, and how often it usually needs us.

      

      

        
Main Street
The long strip center corridor. Owner operated shops, restaurants, tax offices, salons. Lots of dust off the traffic and unpaved lots behind the centers.

        
Bear Valley Road and I Avenue
The busiest intersection in town. Big box adjacent retail, fast food and banks. High traffic doors that want biweekly.

        
Hesperia Road
Older single unit storefronts with original aluminum frames and the most sticker residue we see in town.

        
Ranchero Road
Newer commercial going in around the interchange. First cleans on new glass, then monthly.

        
Industrial parks by the 15
Office fronts on warehouses and yards. Big panes, low foot traffic, monthly is plenty. Dust is the whole problem.

        
Lake Arrowhead Road and the south end
Medical, veterinary and dental suites serving the newer tracts. Interior partitions and waiting rooms.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        How it runs
        
What a Hesperia account looks like

      

      

        
Hesperia's problem is dust. Main Street runs between unpaved lots and open desert, and every afternoon wind puts a film on south facing glass. Glass that sparkles on Monday has a film on it by Friday. Monthly holds most Hesperia storefronts, biweekly holds the busy ones.

        
Because we are based here, a Hesperia account can be added to a route the same week and an urgent call, a tag, a broken sticker job, a pre inspection clean, is usually same day. Nobody is driving up the pass to get to you.

        
Hesperia Road and the older Main Street units often carry decades of adhesive from old signage. That comes off once under adhesive removal, and then the recurring visit is a straightforward clean.

        FAQ
        
Commercial questions from Hesperia

      


      

        

          What does storefront window cleaning cost in Hesperia?
          
$149 flat for storefront glass inside and out, 8 to 10 panes with doors included. Monthly visits are 15% off at $126.65 and every 2 weeks is 25% off at $111.75. Interior partitions and mirrors add $50. Larger fronts and multi unit centers are counted on a free walkthrough and priced firm.

        

        

          How fast can you start?
          
Usually the same week. We are based in Hesperia and a new Main Street or Bear Valley account slots onto an existing evening route. Urgent single visits can often be same day.

        

        

          Do you clean after hours in Hesperia?
          
Yes. After close for restaurants and salons, after five for offices, weekends for anything that cannot close. You choose.

        

        

          We are a small owner run shop. Is monthly enough?
          
For most Main Street shops, yes. If you are on a high traffic door at Bear Valley and I, biweekly is the honest answer because fingerprints and dust film outrun a monthly visit. We tell you which on the walkthrough and you decide.

        

        

          Can you remove old vinyl lettering from a previous tenant?
          
Yes. Adhesive and vinyl removal starts at $129 for up to three panes and is usually done on the first visit, before the recurring clean starts. Hesperia Road units almost always need it.
