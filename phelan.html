        Phelan
        
Four thousand feet changes everything

      

      

        
Phelan sits at about 4,100 feet against the San Gabriels and spreads across roughly sixty square miles of mostly rural parcels. It is a different climate from the valley floor and the work reflects that.

        
The elevation means real temperature swings. Materials expand and contract far more than they do down in Victorville, and that cycling is what finally splits old screen mesh and works sealant loose around flashing. It is why we use All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh up here as the default rather than the upgrade.

        
Almost everything is on well water through the Sheep Creek district or a private well, and it is hard. Left to dry on glass it leaves mineral spotting nothing in a bottle will remove, so purified water is not optional on a Phelan job.

        
The parcels are big and spread out, so we group Phelan work into the same days. If you are flexible about which day, we can almost always fit you into a run that is already headed up the hill.

        FAQ
        
Phelan questions

      

      

        

          Do you actually come up to Phelan?
          
Yes, regularly. We group Phelan and Piñon Hills work onto the same days so the drive is spread across several jobs instead of landing on one customer. Tell us when you call and we will tell you which day we are already coming up.

        

        

          Is there a trip charge for being this far out?
          
No. We just ask for a little flexibility on the day so we can pair you with other stops up here. If you need a specific day we will still do it, the price does not change.

        

        

          Why does my glass spot so badly?
          
Well water in this area is hard. When it dries on glass the minerals stay behind. We wash and rinse with purified deionized water so there is nothing left to dry into a spot.

        

        

          Is all-weather mesh really worth it up here?
          
At four thousand feet, yes. The daily temperature swing works standard mesh loose and splits it years earlier than it would fail down in the valley. We fit all-weather as standard in Phelan for that reason.
