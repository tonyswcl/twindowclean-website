        Why It Matters Out Here
        
The desert gives you sun, then covers it in dust

        

          There's a reason solar panel cleaning matters more in the High Desert than at the coast.
          Wind pushes fine grit off open land and unpaved roads, it settles on your glass, and unlike
          wetter parts of the state, we get almost no rain to wash it off again.
        

      


      

        

          What makes it easy to ignore is that a dirty panel looks fine from the ground. Nothing breaks,
          no light comes on, no alarm goes off. Output just drifts downward month over month, and unless
          you're watching your production data, you won't notice. Most homeowners we clean for have never
          opened their inverter app.
        

        

          How much output dust actually costs is worth being honest about. Independent research consistently
          finds soiling reduces production, and the size of the loss depends on how dirty the panels are and
          how long since the last rain. Low single-digit percentages are normal for lightly soiled panels;
          heavy buildup, bird droppings or a full season of desert dust push it substantially higher.
          We won't quote a specific number for your roof, because we'd be guessing. Check your own production
          history after a cleaning and the panels will tell you themselves.
        

        

          If we notice nesting or debris while cleaning, we explain what is visible and whether a separate pigeon-exclusion assessment would help. Solar cleaning does not include electrical testing or a structural inspection.
        

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        How We Clean
        
The method matters as much as the visit

        
Panel glass has an anti-reflective coating. The wrong approach damages it permanently.

      


      

        
Purified, deionized water
Tap water out here is hard. It dries into mineral spots that block light, which defeats the point. Our water is filtered and deionized so it dries with nothing left behind. No soap, no detergent, no residue for the next round of dust to stick to.

        
Soft brushes, water-fed poles
Never abrasive pads, never harsh chemicals, never a pressure washer. Cleaning follows the quoted method; your manufacturer or provider determines its own warranty requirements.

        
Visible concerns reported
We report visible concerns noticed while cleaning and document them as appropriate. This does not include electrical testing or a structural or mounting certification.

      


      
      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        Timing
        
How often should panels be cleaned?

      

      
    
        
  • 
          
          Twice a year for most High Desert homes
          Spring after the windy season, fall before the shorter days do the work. That rhythm keeps the film from building up without paying for visits you do not need.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
          More often near dust sources
          Unpaved roads, active construction, agricultural land, or birds on the roof. Any of those and quarterly starts paying for itself.
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
          Once a year for sheltered properties
          Newer surrounding development and paved streets slow the buildup. The first clean after a long gap always takes longest.

        FAQ
        
Solar cleaning questions

      


      

        

          How much does solar panel cleaning cost?
          
The $7 rate applies to eligible cleaning. Safe access, panel count and the applicable visit minimum must be confirmed. Your written quote states the scope and total before work begins. The panel wash already included with pigeon proofing is not charged again.

        

        

          Will you damage my panels?
          
No. Soft brush, purified water, water-fed pole. No pressure washing, no abrasives, no chemicals. Panel glass has an anti-reflective coating and we treat it accordingly.

        

        

          Can I just hose my solar panels off?
          
It moves dust around and leaves hard water spots that block light. Tap water out here is mineral heavy and it dries onto the glass. Our water is filtered and deionized so it dries with nothing left behind.

        

        

          Should I clean my solar panels myself?
          
Roof work has real risk and panels are easy to damage. If you do, use soft materials, no pressure, and go up early in the morning when the glass is cool. Wet panels in full sun can crack from thermal shock.

        

        

          How often should solar panels be cleaned in the High Desert?
          
Twice a year suits most High Desert homes: spring after the windy season, fall before the shorter days. More often near unpaved roads, construction, agricultural land, or if you have birds on the roof.

        

        

          Do you clean ground mount arrays?
          
Yes, and they are usually quicker and cheaper since there is no roof access involved.

        

        

          How do I know if my panels need cleaning?
          
Look at your production data over the last few months and compare to the same period last year. If output is drifting down without a good reason, that's your answer.
