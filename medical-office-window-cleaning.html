        What is different
        
How a clinic visit is run

      

      

        
Quiet hours only: before the first patient or after the last, agreed with your office manager. Purified water and a squeegee, no ammonia and no scented cleaners, so the waiting room does not smell of anything when you open. Partitions and sneeze guards are wiped on both sides and the edges dried so there is no streak at eye level from a chair.

        
Exam room sidelights and interior doors are done with the door open and closed again behind us. Nothing is moved that we did not ask about first, and every crew member is the same person you met on the walkthrough.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Price
        
What it costs

      

      

        
$10 a pane inside and out for the exterior glass, with interior partitions and sneeze guards counted as panes at the same rate. A single story clinic with 30 exterior panes and 12 interior pieces is $420. Two story buildings add $10 a pane on the upper floor. The walkthrough is free and the price is confirmed in writing before the first visit.

        
Most clinics run monthly for the entry and interior glass and quarterly for the rest. One invoice at month end and a certificate of general liability insurance on file.

        FAQ
        
Medical office window questions

      

      

        

          Can you clean while we are open?
          
We schedule before the first patient or after the last, whichever your office manager prefers. Exterior glass can be done from outside during the day without anyone coming in.

        

        

          Do you clean the sneeze guards and partitions?
          
Yes, both sides, edges dried, counted as panes at $10 each alongside the windows. That is usually the glass patients look through the most.

        

        

          What do you clean with?
          
Purified water and a squeegee. No ammonia, no scented products, nothing that leaves an odor in a waiting room or a residue on the glass.

        

        

          How much does it cost?
          
$10 a pane inside and out, interior partitions counted the same way, $10 more a pane on an upper story. A free walkthrough sets the count and the price is confirmed in writing.

        

        

          Do you have insurance we can keep on file?
          
Yes. General liability insurance is in place and a certificate is sent before the first visit.
