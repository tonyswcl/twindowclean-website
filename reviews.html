Behind the stars

What the reviews do not say, but should

  • Everything is in writing first Inspection is free, the quote is firm, and it is written before anything starts. If the scope changes on the roof we stop and ask. Nobody has ever had to review us about a surprise bill.
  • Clips, never bolts, under solar arrays Bolting mesh through a panel frame voids most panel warranties and tears loose in High Desert wind. Ours is clipped. That is why we can put a lifetime workmanship warranty on it.
  • Purified water on every pane and panel Deionized water dries with nothing left behind. No film for the next dust storm to grab. It is a slower setup than a hose and a squeegee and it is the reason the clean lasts.
  • The owner answers Call 714-559-0300 and you get the person who will be on your roof. Not a call center, not a scheduler reading a script.

        FAQ
        
Review questions

      


      

        

          Are these real reviews?
          
Yes. Every one is copied from our Google Business Profile as written, spelling included. The Google listing has all 25 and you can read them there without taking our word for it.

        

        

          Do you pay or discount for reviews?
          
No. We ask every customer once after the job, by text, with the link. That is it. Paid or incentivized reviews break Google's rules and would put the listing at risk, which is worth more to us than any single review.

        

        

          Why are there not more reviews if you have been doing this a while?
          
Because for a long time we did not ask. Most of the work came from referrals and repeat customers and nobody thought to send the link. We ask now, every job, and the count moves every month.

        

        

          Where else can I check you out?
          
Yelp has a listing under Tony's Window Cleaning, Hesperia. Facebook at facebook.com/twindowclean has job photos going back years. And the gallery page here is all our own work, with the city named on each photo.
