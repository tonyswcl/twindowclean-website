The towns

Where the route days go

Palmdale

Rancho Vista, Anaverde and the tracts along Avenue S and Rancho Vista Boulevard. Two story homes with large south facing arrays, and the wind off the hills that coats west glass in a week.

Lancaster and Quartz Hill

What we do here

The same four services, the same prices

Window cleaning

$149 single story, $249 two story, outside, with screens, tracks and sills included. Inside every window is $49 more. Purified water, so the glass dries with nothing on it.

Solar panel cleaning

$7 a panel with purified water and a soft brush. Nobody walks on your panels, and if we notice anything visible while we are up there, like a cracked panel or birds, we point it out. Solar cleaning in Palmdale and Lancaster has the details.

Pigeon proofing

$450 for up to 12 panels, $50 a panel past that, $125 more on a two story home. Rigid steel mesh clipped to the frame, never drilled, with 2 to 3 spinners, the cleanout and a panel wash included. Pigeon proofing in Palmdale and Lancaster walks through the job.

Window screens

Re-meshed at the truck from $53.99, All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh $64.99, Full Upgrade from $84.99. Added to a window cleaning there is no separate screen minimum, and two screens take a single story window visit past the $249 line.

Everything else we do

The far rule

What $249 means and how to reach it

Pigeon proofing meets the minimum on its own, and so does a two story window cleaning at $249. A single story window cleaning at $149 does not, and a 20 panel solar wash at $140 does not either, so Antelope Valley visits are usually two services on one stop: windows and solar together at $289, windows and a few screens, or a solar wash on a bigger array.
