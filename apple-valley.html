        Apple Valley
        
Half of Apple Valley should not be on a ladder

      

      

        
Apple Valley has roughly five thousand age-restricted homes across its 55+ communities, and that changes what this job actually is. A lot of our customers here are not deciding whether to hire someone, they are deciding who. Getting up a ladder is not on the table.

        
Jess Ranch alone is over two thousand homes, and Sun City and Solera add most of another two thousand. These are well-kept properties where the owners simply cannot do the roof and gutter work themselves anymore, and they want somebody who shows up when he says he will and does not surprise them with a bill.

        
Desert Knolls is the opposite end of the town. Bigger lots, older houses, more trees, and gutters that fill with debris and then start attracting nesting.

        
Out toward Bell Mountain the properties get rural and exposed. More wind, more dust on the panels, and longer runs of roof edge to seal when the pigeons find it.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        Neighborhoods
        
Apple Valley neighborhoods we work in

        
A lot of our Apple Valley work is repeat business in the same handful of communities.

      

      

        

          
Jess Ranch

          
Over two thousand homes in a 55+ community. Mostly scheduled maintenance work for people who would rather not be on a ladder, and we keep the same visit day so it is predictable.

        

        

          
Sun City Apple Valley

          
Del Webb community, around seventeen hundred homes. Consistent floor plans, which means we can usually quote accurately over the phone before we even come out.

        

        

          
Solera at Del Webb

          
Another age-restricted community. Same story as Sun City, and a lot of our customers here came from a neighbor's recommendation.

        

        

          
Desert Knolls and Upper Desert Knolls

          
Larger lots, older housing stock, mature trees. Gutter cleanouts and roof debris are the recurring jobs here.

        

        

          
Bell Mountain Estates

          
Rural and exposed. Panels dust over fast and there is more roof perimeter to seal against birds.

        

        

          
Apple Valley Country Club

          
Course-side sprinkler overspray means hard water on the glass, same as the Hesperia club streets.

        FAQ
        
Apple Valley questions

      

      

        

          Do you work inside Jess Ranch and Sun City?
          
Constantly. A good share of our Apple Valley work is repeat maintenance inside the 55+ communities. We keep the same visit day where we can so it stays predictable, and we are used to signing in at the gate.

        

        

          I cannot get on a ladder anymore. Do you do everything?
          
Yes. Exterior glass, screens, tracks, sills, gutters, solar and roof work all get done from our equipment, not yours. You do not need to move anything or be outside while we work.

        

        

          Can you give me a price over the phone?
          
In Sun City and Solera, usually yes, because the floor plans repeat and we have done a lot of them. Anywhere else we would rather come look, and the look is free.

        

        

          Do you offer a plan so I am not calling every few months?
          
That is most of what we do out here. A plan fixes the price for the year, schedules the visits in advance, and works out cheaper per visit than booking one at a time.
