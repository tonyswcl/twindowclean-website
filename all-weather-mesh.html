All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh

For sun-facing windows, this exterior shading material is specified to block up to 80% of solar heat and glare under the manufacturer’s product specifications. It is durable vinyl-coated polyester, pet-resistant, fade-resistant and mildew-resistant, with insect-screening functionality. Clean with mild soap and water.

It provides additional shade and may improve comfort. The denser weave changes outward visibility, airflow and incoming light compared with standard insect screening. Results depend on the opening, exposure and installation; this is not an 80% reduction in room temperature, total home heat or utility bills. Pet-resistant does not mean pet-proof.

We confirm the material for your quoted work. Manufacturer material specifications are separate from any installation coverage in your written quote.

A screen that fits, with an even finish

A good screen needs a frame that fits the opening and mesh held at an even tension. We check the frame, corners and retaining clips, then recommend re-meshing a sound frame or replacing a bent or missing frame. Choose charcoal fiberglass or the heavier vinyl-coated polyester all-weather material. We explain the view, airflow and daylight tradeoffs before quoting the work.

Do I need new mesh or a new frame?

A straight frame with sound corners may only need new mesh and spline. Bent, damaged or missing frames need a different scope. Your written quote identifies re-meshing and frame replacement separately.

Is all-weather mesh pet-proof, and is there a minimum?

All-weather mesh is a material option, not a promise that pets cannot damage it. Screen-only visits have the existing $149 minimum; discounts do not reduce that floor. Adding screen work to another service is priced in that quote.

Material choice and even tension

Slightly heavier weave

You give up a little airflow and a little clarity against charcoal fiberglass. On a shaded north window we will tell you to keep fiberglass and save the $11.

Where it belongs

Which windows should get it

South and west facing windows in Hesperia, Victorville, Apple Valley and Adelanto, without question. Those take the full afternoon sun nine months a year and every builder screen we pull from them is brittle.

Any window a dog or cat sits behind. Sliding door screens and low bedroom windows are the usual ones. Pet claws go through fiberglass fast, and vinyl coated polyester holds up far better.

Homes on the open desert in Phelan, Oak Hills and the Mesa, where wind carries grit that sandblasts a screen over a season. The heavier strand shrugs it off.

Honest trade offs

What All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh is not

It is not invisible. Charcoal fiberglass is the clearest mesh made, and if a picture window with a view is your priority, that is the one to keep. We re-mesh plenty of homes with fiberglass on the view side and all weather everywhere else.

The material is pet-resistant, not pet-proof. A pet can still damage mesh or bend a frame. We assess the opening and explain material options and tradeoffs before quoting.

        FAQ
        
All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh questions

      

      

        

          Is all weather mesh the same as pet screen?
          
Same family of material that stores sell as pet screen, heavy duty or all weather. Vinyl coated polyester is what matters. Ours is the heavy grade, not the thin version some hardware stores carry.

        

        

          Can I see through it as well as regular screen?
          
Almost. The strands are a little thicker so the view is a touch darker, most people stop noticing within a day. On a window where the view is the whole point, keep charcoal fiberglass there and put all weather on the sun side.

        

        

          How long does it last up here?
Screen-only work uses two appointments: pickup and inspection, then a separate confirmed return for drop-off, installation and only the cleaning in your written agreement. You can request a return date or let Tony confirm it after inspection. Requested dates are not confirmed bookings; pickup is not completed work. Combined-service arrangements are confirmed for the job.

        

          Do you carry it on the truck?
          
We bring the materials agreed for your job. On-site work or pickup, return and reinstallation are confirmed before booking, including the expected turnaround.

        

        

          Is it worth the extra $11 a screen?
          
Material choice depends on exposure, use, view and airflow. We explain the options at the window without guaranteeing a specific lifespan or that pets cannot damage it.
