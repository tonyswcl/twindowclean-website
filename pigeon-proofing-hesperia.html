        Neighborhoods
        
What is different about Hesperia

      

      

        
Most Hesperia arrays we proof sit on concrete tile, which we can walk carefully, and the roof pitch on the Ranchero tracts is usually gentle enough that we work from the deck rather than off ladders. That keeps the day shorter and the price down.

        
The thing that catches people here is how far the droppings travel. Pass wind moves the lighter material off the panel area and onto the tile below, so by the time you notice staining on the roof the nesting has usually been going a year or more. We quote the tile wash with the exclusion because separating them makes no sense.

        
No trip charge anywhere in Hesperia, and no charge to come out and look.

        FAQ
        
Pigeon Proofing questions

      

      

        

          Do you cover Hesperia?
          
Yes. Hesperia is home base, not a service area we drive out to, so we are here most days of the week. We work across The Mesa, the Ranchero corridor, Hesperia Golf & Country Club, Downtown Hesperia, Summit Valley and Mesa Linda. No trip charge, and no charge to come and look.

        

        

          What does pigeon proofing cost in Hesperia?
          
Hesperia jobs mostly land between $450 and $700. The Ranchero corridor arrays run larger and push toward the upper end, while older single story homes near Main tend to sit at the bottom of it.

        

        

          Do you install bird spinners?
          
Yes, and they are free. Every pigeon proofing job includes 2 to 3 reflective spinners, depending on the size of the home. They flash and turn in the wind and make the open roof less inviting to land on, which takes pressure off the mesh. The mesh keeps pigeons out from under the panels; the spinners make them less likely to land on the roof at all.

        

        

          Will the tile staining come off too?
          
Usually most of it. Droppings are acidic and if they have sat on Hesperia tile for a year or more some of the discolouration has gone into the surface. We wash and sanitize the area as part of the job and tell you honestly what will not come back.

        

        

          Do you drill into the roof or the panels?
          
No. We clip the mesh to the panel frame. Bolting through mesh puts holes in the material and sometimes the frame, which can void your solar warranty, and out here the wind works it loose off the bolt heads within a couple of seasons. Clips hold it without a single hole.
