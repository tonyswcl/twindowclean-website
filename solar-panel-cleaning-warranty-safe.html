Solar Cleaning That Keeps Your Warranty

Purified water, a soft brush, and feet that never touch a panel. $7 a panel.

Every panel maker publishes cleaning rules, and they all say the same three things: no abrasives, no harsh chemicals, no pressure washers. Most of them also say not to walk on the modules. Our method is built around those rules, which is why the warranty question has a short answer.

The method

What touches your panels and what does not

Purified water only

Deionized water carries no minerals, so it dries with nothing left behind. No soap, no rinse aid, no chemicals that could react with the anti reflective coating.

Soft brush on a pole

A soft bristle brush on a water fed pole, worked from the roof edge or the ground. No scrapers, no scouring pads, no pressure washer.

Nobody on the modules

We work from the roof deck beside the array, not on it. Standing on a panel can microcrack cells that still look fine from the ground.

What the rules say

What installers and panel makers ask for

The maintenance sections of the big manufacturers' manuals read almost alike: clean with water and a soft cloth or brush, no abrasive materials, no high pressure, avoid cleaning a hot panel with cold water. Some installers add their own line about not walking on the array. We follow all of it, and we clean early in the morning when panels are cool for the same reason the manuals give.

What voids warranties in practice is damage: cracked glass from a boot, scratched coating from a scrub pad, a corroded connector from a pressure washer forcing water into a junction box. None of those happen with a soft brush and purified water.

What you get

The visit, and what we look for while we are up there

Every panel washed row by row and left to dry spot free. Nothing to wipe, because purified water leaves no minerals. If we notice anything visible while we are there, like cracked glass, lifted flashing or birds under the panels, we point it out. Birds under the panels are the most common thing we find.

If we find something, you get a photo and a plain explanation. If it is birds, pigeon proofing includes the panel wash free, so nothing you paid today is wasted.

        FAQ
        
Warranty and cleaning questions

      

      

        

          Will cleaning my solar panels void the warranty?
          
Not the way we do it. Manufacturers ask for water, a soft brush, no abrasives, no chemicals and no pressure washing, and that is our whole method. What voids warranties is damage, and a soft brush with purified water does not cause any.

        

        

          Do you walk on the panels?
          
No. We work from the roof deck beside the array with a water fed pole. Stepping on a module can microcrack cells that still look perfect from the ground.

        

        

          Why purified water instead of tap water?
          
Tap water up here is hard. It dries into white mineral spots that block light, which is the problem you were trying to fix. Deionized water has nothing in it, so the panel dries clear with nothing to wipe.

        

        

          Do you use soap or any cleaner?
          
No. Soap leaves a film and some cleaners react with anti reflective coatings. Purified water and agitation from the brush lift desert dust and bird droppings without anything else.

        

        

          Can you tell if there is damage while cleaning?
          
Yes, anything we can see while we clean: cracked glass, lifted flashing or birds nesting under the array. We point it out. We do not inspect or repair the electrical system. You get a photo and a straight answer, not a sales pitch.
