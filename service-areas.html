Where we are every week

Tap a city for what we usually find there, real job photos, and the local page for each service.

Hesperia

Home base. Fastest response in the area, and the town with the most solar arrays per street we work on. Heavy pigeon pressure on the newer tracts south of Main Street.

Hesperia overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens · Storefronts

Victorville

Biggest city up here and the least consistent. Lakefront hard water at Spring Valley Lake, big arrays off Bear Valley, original windows in Old Town, and most of our commercial storefront accounts.

Victorville overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens · Storefronts

Apple Valley

Larger lots, more custom homes, more two story roofs. Jess Ranch and the Town Center corridor carry most of our medical and dental storefront work.

Apple Valley overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens · Storefronts

Adelanto

Dry lake dust is the story. Panels film over faster than anywhere else we work and thin screen mesh does not last, which is why we push All-Weather Solar-Control Mesh here.

Adelanto overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens

Oak Hills

Rural lots, well water, wind straight off the Cajon Pass. Arrays are big, roofs are steep, and hard water on the glass is close to universal.

Oak Hills overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens

Phelan

Spread out and high. Long driveways, outbuildings, well water and constant grit. We batch Phelan jobs so nobody pays for the drive.

Phelan overview · Windows · Solar · Pigeons · Screens

Spring Valley Lake

A community in the Victor Valley where lake irrigation can leave mineral deposits on glass. Tell us about your property so we can confirm the service scope and scheduling.

Spring Valley Lake page · Windows · Hard water · Pigeons

Silverwood

The master planned community going up in Hesperia's Summit Valley. New homes, solar on nearly every roof, and construction dust for years to come. Its own place, with its own page.

Silverwood page · Windows · Solar · New home clean

Fontana

Our regular stop down the hill. Mostly solar cleaning and pigeon proofing, at the same prices as up here and with no trip fee.

Pigeons · Solar

Helendale, Silver Lakes and Baldy Mesa

Part of our regular area. Lake and well irrigation in Helendale and Silver Lakes leaves mineral deposits on low panes. Baldy Mesa is high plateau work above Phelan, with more pine debris in gutters and on panels.

Further out

Places we reach on a planned day

Barstow, Lucerne Valley and Piñon Hills are by request. We group jobs on a scheduled route day so the drive is shared and the price stays on the list.

Wrightwood and the Cajon Pass are by request, mountain and high plateau work. Cooler, fewer pigeons, a lot more pine debris in gutters and on panels.

Crestline, Lake Arrowhead, Running Springs and Big Bear Lake are by request, grouped mountain days at the same prices, with a $149 job minimum and no trip fee. Each town has its own page, starting at mountain communities.

Palmdale, Lancaster and the Antelope Valley are by request on grouped route days, about an hour west of us on Highway 138. Same prices, no trip fee, and a $249 job minimum this far out: pigeon proofing meets it on its own, windows or solar alone usually pair with another service. Tony confirms every Antelope Valley visit personally before it goes on the calendar.

Yucca Valley, Joshua Tree and the Morongo Basin are by request the same way, down Highway 247 through Lucerne Valley. Same $249 minimum, same prices, no trip fee, and your requested day stays a request until Tony texts to confirm it. Solar panel cleaning in Yucca Valley is the first service page there.

Helendale, Silver Lakes and Spring Valley Lake are part of our regular area and share one thing: lake or well irrigation that leaves mineral deposits on every low pane. If you are in any of these, read the hard water page before you call, because a standard wash will not fix what you are looking at.

Inland Empire

Down the hill, same prices

We started with Inland Empire customers and still take the calls. Fontana is part of our regular area. Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Upland, Chino Hills, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Corona, Norco, Rialto, San Bernardino, Highland, Redlands, Yucaipa and San Jacinto are by request, mostly solar and pigeon work, grouped onto a scheduled route day.

The IE is a different market. Denser tracts, more HOA rules about what can be visible on a roof, and less hard water than the desert. The price list is the same, with no travel fee. Cities, prices and how a job down the hill works are all on our Inland Empire page.

        FAQ
        
Service area questions

      


      

        

          Do you charge a trip fee to come out?
          
No. There is no trip fee anywhere we work. Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley, Adelanto, Oak Hills, Phelan, Fontana, Helendale, Silver Lakes and Baldy Mesa are our regular area. Further out, by request, we group jobs on a scheduled route day, so the date may be a few days out, but nothing is added for distance.

        

        

          Is the price the same in every city?
          
Yes. What changes the number is the house, not the zip code. Single or two story, how many windows, how many panels, whether the glass has hard water etching. Each city page explains what we usually run into in that town, but the starting prices on this page apply everywhere.

        

        

          Do you still work in the Inland Empire?
          
Yes, by request on scheduled route days: Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Upland, Chino Hills, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Riverside, Corona, Norco, Rialto, San Bernardino, Highland, Redlands, Yucaipa and San Jacinto, mostly pigeon and solar work, at the same prices as up here with no travel fee. Fontana is part of our regular area. See the Inland Empire page for more.

        

        

          What if my city is not listed?
          
Call anyway. If it is within a reasonable drive of Hesperia we can almost always make it work, and we will say so honestly if we cannot.

        

        

          Do you come to Palmdale, Lancaster or Yucca Valley?
          
By request, on grouped route days. The Antelope Valley and the Morongo Basin are 60 to 95 miles from Hesperia, so a visit needs $249 of work to be worth the drive: pigeon proofing meets it on its own, and windows, solar or screens usually get there together. Same prices as at home, no trip fee. Your requested day stays a request until Tony texts to confirm it. Details on the Antelope Valley and Morongo Basin pages.

        

        

          Why do you have a separate page for each city?
          
Because the work is different. Spring Valley Lake glass is etched by lake irrigation, Adelanto arrays are dusted by the dry lake, Phelan homes are on well water and spread out on big lots. A page that treats them all the same would be lying to somebody. Each city page carries real photos from jobs in that city.
